Stahl Capital has one client.

You. It tracks your portfolio, reviews it against your own policy and simulates rebalancing, all from plain files on your machine. A native app, with no web view and no account.

Department 01 The Vault

Your data is a text file.

No proprietary format and no export button to beg for. Positions live in JSON you can read, diff and back up however you already back things up. The same files work with portfolio_rs in a terminal.

    Department 03 Compliance
    
          
    A policy you wrote, enforced by someone with no opinions.
    
          
    
            Put your targets and limits in a machine-readable policy once. Review then checks drift, constraints and data
            quality against it every time, and tells you what needs doing. Policy and Review live in a workspace, which is
            a folder like any other.
          
    
        
    
        
    
          
          
    # portfolio/policy.toml
name = "Balanced Growth"
risk_profile = "moderate"

[constraints]
minimum_cash_amount = 20000.0
single_position_limit_percent = 40.0

[[allocations]]
asset_class = "Stocks"
target_percent = 50.0
tolerance_percent = 5.0
